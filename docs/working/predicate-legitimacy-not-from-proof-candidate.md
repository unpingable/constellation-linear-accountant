## The seam

A proof / credential / score *satisfies* a predicate; it does not *authorize* it.
Privacy hides the subject; it does not legitimate the category. The chain that
keeps these apart: declaration → witness → admission → standing → **spend**.

## LA's role and refusal

The Linear Accountant is the **spend plane**. It consumes already-admitted,
already-standing authority for a bounded, exactly-once effect. Its refusal here is
the same shape as AG's existing never-do list (no mint from an ALLOW verdict, no
regeneration from re-validation), one substrate out:

> **The spend plane never reads a proof and infers predicate legitimacy.** A
> satisfaction proof — ZK or otherwise — is not a capacity grant and not an
> admissibility verdict. LA spends what Standing already made spendable against a
> Wicket-admitted, witnessed predicate; it does not look at the proof and decide
> the category was real.

`standing ≠ spend` and `proof-satisfaction ≠ spendable-authority` are the links
LA holds. If the spend plane ever starts treating "the subject proved P" as
grounds to release capacity, that is the laundering the whole chain exists to
refuse. Cite the AG canonical note for the full chain and plane map. No LA change
is implied; this is a handle for review (and consistent with AG's hands-off,
named-consumer posture toward LA).
